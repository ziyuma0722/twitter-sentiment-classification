                     learning rate        batch size          optimizer               regularization                scheduler                    other
Pretraining:   relative big         relative big          AdamW             Weight decay (0.01)         linear / cosine       warmup, clipping
Finetuning:    relative small      relative small       AdamW             Weight decay (0.01 - 0.1)  often linear decay   early stopping optional, PEFT sometimes

Finetuning:
Limited data, enough compute:  full fine-tuning, to prevent overfitting (early stopping, AdamW with weight decay, dropout)
Limited data, limited compute: PEFT (LoRA, Adapters, Prefix Tuning), already less overfitting due to fewer trainable parameters, can be combined with AdamW (fewer weight decay), early stopping

In LLM pretraining: AdamW optimizer, weight decay, learning rate warm-up + cosine/linear decay, mixed precision trainig, gradient clipping,
no dropout in the transformer, but sometimes LayerNorm and embedding dropout
